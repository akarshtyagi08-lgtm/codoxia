Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect essential information required to maintain your developer profile, such as your email address, submission history, and challenge activity.

2. In-Browser Tool Privacy

All developer tools (JSON Formatter, Regex Evaluator, Base64 Encoder) process payloads locally in client memory. Your raw inputs are never logged or stored on our servers.

3. Security Standards

Account data and authentication state are secured via Supabase encrypted sessions. We do not sell user data to third-party advertisers.
